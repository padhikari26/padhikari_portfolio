About Me

I am a high school senior passionate about exploring technology, solving problems, and creating tools that make a real-world impact. From AI research to community apps, my projects combine curiosity, persistence, and a desire to learn and grow.

Each experience — whether conducting COVID-19 data analysis, teaching programming to students overseas, or building community-focused apps — has shaped my understanding of technology and how it can empower people and communities.

My Journey

I moved to the United States at age seven and started school in first grade. Computers quickly became my passion — from Scratch mini-games and Code.org challenges to creating full websites and exploring AI. Each project pushed me to learn new technical skills and think creatively.

My COVID-19 research used public datasets to analyze infection trends and outcomes across countries. Presenting findings at a symposium taught me not only technical skills like data cleaning, analysis, and visualization, but also how to communicate insights to diverse audiences.

To share knowledge, I founded a Python Bootcamp, teaching students overseas who are just beginning their coding journey. I also launched NAFA Samaj, a community app helping people connect, share resources, and engage with one another.

Outside of technology, I play club soccer with EagleFC, learning leadership, teamwork, and persistence — lessons I bring back to my technical projects and research.

I’m excited to pursue Computer Science in college, combining academic rigor with hands-on projects that create impact, while continuing to learn and inspire others.
