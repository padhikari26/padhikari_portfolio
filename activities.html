Extracurriculars

Activities & Leadership

What I do outside class—places where I learn to listen, lead, and contribute to community.

EagleFC — Midfield

Soccer has been my reset button and my classroom. Playing midfield taught me to read the field, communicate quickly, and make decisions under pressure. Our club placed in regional tournaments (placeholder placements), but what I’m most proud of is how our team stayed composed and supported each other in tough moments.

What I learned: resilience, situational awareness, and the discipline to show up even when it’s hard.

Nepali Student Association — Co-Planner

I help plan events that bring together Nepalese students and friends to share culture, language, and food. My role includes scheduling, coordinating announcements, and making sure new students feel welcome. Seeing people find community reminds me why organizing matters.

What I learned: inclusive planning, clear communication, and how small details shape a welcoming space.

Envirothon — Co-Leader

As co-leader, I helped my team prepare across soils, forestry, wildlife, and aquatics. We placed 2nd overall at the county level, which felt meaningful because we divided topics, taught each other, and stayed consistent with practice.

What I learned: collaborative study habits, presenting technical info simply, and staying curious about systems beyond CS.

STEM Outreach & Tutoring

I tutor peers in CS and math and run a weekly online Python bootcamp for overseas students (~20 regular attendees). One of my favorite moments was when a student shared an error to the group and another student calmly explained the fix—it showed me that confidence grows when learning is shared.

What I learned: patience, mentorship, and how to build a supportive learning environment.
