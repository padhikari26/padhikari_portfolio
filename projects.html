COVID-19 Data Science Research

Problem: How do vaccination rates and deaths move together across countries, and which policy timings are most associated with better outcomes?

What I built: A 6-week analysis with The Coding School using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib on the Johns Hopkins dataset to visualize time-series trends and intervention timing.

Impact: Earlier quarantines and faster vaccine uptake were associated with fewer deaths and quicker recovery trends; I presented the findings at a student symposium in College Park, MD.

Skills & Growth: Data cleaning, EDA, visualization, and translating technical results for non-technical audiences. Next: add confidence intervals and country clusters for clearer comparisons.

Sustainable Crop Yield Prediction

Problem: Feeding a growing population requires higher yields with lower environmental cost; can soil-microbe data help predict crop output?

What I built: ML models (Linear Regression, KNN, Decision Trees, Random Forest) on Australian soil-bacteria datasets to predict yield; Random Forest performed best in baseline tests.

Impact: Established a viable baseline and roadmap: add moisture, pH, and sunlight features; test expanded ensembles; prune for interpretability; trial on additional farms.

Skills & Growth: Regression modeling, feature importance, evaluation, and framing ML results for real stakeholders.
